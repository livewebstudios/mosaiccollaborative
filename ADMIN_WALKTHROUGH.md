# Posting to Insights: a quick guide for Roz, Deb and Amalia

The Insights page on mosaiccollaborative.com has its own admin. You can add articles, podcast
appearances, LinkedIn and Substack pieces, and conference talks yourself. No code, no waiting on us.

## Logging in

1. You'll get an email invitation from Netlify. Click the link, choose a password, and you land in the admin.
2. After that, go to **mosaiccollaborative.com/admin/** any time and click **Login with Netlify Identity**.
3. Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login box.

## Adding a post

1. Click **Insights** on the left, then **New Insight**.
2. Fill in the fields from top to bottom:
   - **Title**: the headline.
   - **Short description**: one or two sentences for the card and for Google. Keep it under 155 characters.
   - **Date**: the publish date.
   - **Category**: Leadership, Culture, Change, AI at work, or Podcasts & appearances.
   - **Author**: which of you wrote it, or The Mosaic Collaborative.
   - **Body**: the article itself. The toolbar handles headings, bold, lists, links and quotes.
3. Click **Save**.

## Adding a photo

In **Photo (optional)**, click **Choose an image**, then **Upload**, and pick a file from your computer.
Landscape photos work best (wider than tall, at least 1600 pixels across). The photo shows at the top
of the post and on its card.

## Linking to a podcast, LinkedIn post or Substack piece

1. Paste the address into **Link to the original**.
2. In **Link button text**, write what the button should say, for example "Listen to the episode",
   "Read on Substack" or "Event details". Leave it alone and it says "Read the original".
3. If the piece lives somewhere else and you only want a line or two here, keep the body short
   (under about 40 words). The card on the Insights page then links straight to the original.

## Publishing

New posts start as drafts, so nothing goes live by accident. When you're ready:

1. Uncheck **Draft**.
2. Click **Save**, then **Publish** at the top right.

The site updates on its own in about a minute. Refresh the Insights page to see it.

## Editing or removing a post

Open it from the Insights list, make your changes and save. To take a post down without deleting
it, check **Draft** again and save.
